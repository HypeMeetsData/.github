# Hype Meets Data

**Don't trust. Test.**

We test market claims on real data.

Hype Meets Data is an independent research project dedicated to examining popular claims about financial markets, trading strategies, and cryptocurrency using historical data, transparent methodologies, and reproducible experiments.

### What we do

- **Test claims** — turn popular market narratives into measurable research questions.
- **Analyze evidence** — examine historical data, compare outcomes, and test limitations.
- **Publish findings** — share research results, methodology, and supporting evidence.
- **Support reproducibility** — provide references and research materials where available.

### Our approach

**Claim → Data → Experiment → Controls → Evidence → Verdict**

We distinguish observed results from confirmed findings, document limitations, and avoid presenting historical performance as a guarantee of future outcomes.
